No accounts, no sign-ups, nothing you do in a game is sent to me. Here is the
whole of it, in plain words.

## The games

Each game is one file that runs entirely in your browser. While you play, it
talks to nobody: no scores uploaded, no events counted, no requests of any
kind. Your best score is kept by your own browser, on your own device. Clear
your browsing data and it is gone. I never see it, and neither does anyone
else.

## The server

The site is delivered by Amazon CloudFront, and like any web server it writes
a line for each file it hands out: what was asked for, when, your IP address,
and the name your browser gives for itself. I count those lines to see roughly
how many people came by, which games they opened, how long they stayed and
which part of the world they came from — the last one from the city my
delivery network served them out of, which is only ever roughly right. That is the only thing I
do with them, and they are deleted automatically after about thirteen months.
There is no analytics script on this site and no cookie of mine.

## Ads

The site is set up to carry ads from Google AdSense. When they are running,
Google and its advertising partners may read and write cookies or similar
storage in your browser to choose an ad and measure whether it worked. That
part is Google's doing, on Google's terms, not mine, and it is described in
[how Google uses information from sites that use its
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you are in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked first, before any of
that happens, and nothing beyond showing you a plain untargeted ad occurs if
you say no. You can change your answer whenever you like from the link in the
same banner.

## The feedback form

At the bottom of every page there is a button that takes you to a form run by
Tally. It is only a link: until you click it, this site asks Tally for nothing
and Tally is not told you were here. If you do click and fill the form in, what
you typed goes to Tally, along with which page you came from and which
language you were reading, and I read it there. It is an ordinary form, so
please keep anything genuinely private out of it.

## Asking me things

If you want to know what I hold about you, or want it deleted, say so through
the feedback form. The honest answer is likely to be "a few server log lines,
already on their way out", but ask and I will check.
