## The prompt

Saturday, 13:57, right after the water game:

> New game: whack a mole style but you need to hit president Trump or president Putin (game mode choice?) . Look up pictures of their face on internet to use them. They should say stuff with bubbles. Keep a funny tone. Make a plan for our game as usual

Two things in there did not survive: the real names and the real photos. Claude
said no to both in one paragraph — it does not put invented words in a real
person's mouth, and a site that runs ads does not want press photos with fake
speech bubbles. No lecture, though: the replacement came in the same message,
two invented leaders drawn from scratch. **President Tronk**, "The Dealmaker":
orange, tiny hands, a tie that goes on forever. **President Poutine**, "The Strongman": deadpan, ice-blue
eyes. Nine holes, sixty seconds, three modes — including a Summit where the
two insult each other across the grid.

## The iterations

Three versions, four messages from me.

1. The brief, then "Ok let's see". Fourteen minutes later: a 3×3 grid of holes, bubbles, a combo counter, and an end screen called the Official Bonk Report. The loser sulks in his hole: "Rigged. Totally rigged."
2. "It's too easy. Cannot read the text it's small and too fast. The hair of trump is weird. I don't like the Putin character" — heads stay up half as long, escapes cost a point, every line cut to a few words, bubbles bigger and slower to fade. The hair was redrawn; Poutine came back in a dark suit.
3. "The text stays too long and cover the screen. I guess only some head's should have text. Putin is fine this way. Let's spice it up" — a third of the pop-ups now talk, two bubbles at most. Spicing up meant a peace dove and a coffee-carrying intern you must not hit, a golden press conference worth five times, and a FINAL FRENZY at the end.

## What I learned

The best part of the brief was the "no". I asked for real faces and got two
characters of my own instead; the joke works just as well, and a refusal that
arrives with the alternative already built is the only kind worth getting. The
one design call I made — only some heads talk — is what fixed the game.
